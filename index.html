Good to Know

Will servicing my car here void my factory warranty?

No. Federal law lets you service your car at any independent shop. We use OEM-spec parts and fluids and log every service so your warranty stays intact.

How long does a typical repair take?

Oil changes take about 40 minutes. Most brake jobs and diagnostics are done the same day. For bigger repairs we give you a time estimate with the quote.

Do you offer a warranty on repairs?

Yes. Parts and labor are covered for 24 months or 24,000 miles, whichever comes first.

Can I wait while my car is serviced?

Of course. Our waiting room has Wi-Fi, coffee and charging stations. For longer jobs we offer a free shuttle within 5 miles.
